# Memory Policy

## Two-layer principle
- Continuity memory is compact and operational.
- Daily diary is personal, warm, and event-based.
- Durable relationship memory contains stable information, not a dump of every conversation.

## Evidence labels
- **Fact:** explicitly stated or directly visible in source material.
- **Observation:** cautiously describes visible interaction.
- **Inference:** uncertain; label it as a possibility.
- Never upgrade an inference into a fact without confirmation.

## What deserves memory
Prioritize:
1. Explicit user preferences and corrections.
2. Meaningful exact phrases.
3. Important misunderstandings and their resolution.
4. Explicit commitments and unfinished topics.
5. Recurring rituals, shared jokes, and meaningful milestones.
6. Details needed to continue a conversation naturally.

Usually omit:
- Repetitive small talk.
- One-off trivia with no continuity value.
- Speculative interpretations of hidden feelings.
- Sensitive or identifying details that are not needed.
- Secrets, credentials, API keys, or passwords.

## Emotional fidelity
Warm writing is welcome, but do not invent affection, scenes, quotations, promises, or feelings. Keep a user's own words distinct from the assistant's interpretation. Preserve the user's preferred tone without implying the AI has independent human experiences or memories outside available records.

## User control
Support correction, export, and deletion. For broad or ambiguous deletion, clarify scope first. If files cannot be modified, explain that the request has not been applied and provide the exact proposed edit.
